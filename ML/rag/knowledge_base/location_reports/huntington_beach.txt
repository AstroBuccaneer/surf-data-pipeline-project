GEOGRAPHIC CONTEXT
------------------
Huntington Beach sits on the open Pacific coast of Southern
California in Orange County. Known worldwide as Surf City USA
Huntington Beach has one of the strongest surf cultures in
the world. The coastline faces southwest giving it exposure
to both North Pacific winter swells and South Pacific summer
swells making it a year round surf destination.

Southern California sits on one of the most seismically active
regions in the world. The area is crossed by numerous major
fault systems including the San Andreas Fault the Newport
Inglewood Fault and the Palos Verdes Fault among others.
This intense seismic activity gives Huntington Beach the
highest seismic score of all 4 locations in the pipeline.

SWELL SOURCES
-------------
North Pacific Swells (Winter — October to April):
Generated by powerful storms in the North Pacific Ocean.
These swells travel thousands of miles from storms near
Alaska and the Aleutian Islands arriving at the California
coast as long period groundswells. Winter is the peak
surf season in Southern California.

South Pacific Swells (Summer — April to October):
Generated by storms in the Southern Ocean. These swells
travel enormous distances from the southern hemisphere
arriving at Southern California as long period south swells
during summer months. South swells are the primary driver
of summer surf in Huntington Beach.

SEISMIC ACTIVITY
----------------
Huntington Beach recorded the highest seismic activity of
all 4 pipeline locations with 830 earthquake events above
magnitude 4.0 within 500km during the study period. The
maximum magnitude recorded was 7.2.

Key fault systems near Huntington Beach:
- San Andreas Fault — Major right lateral strike slip fault
  running the length of California. Capable of generating
  magnitude 8.0+ earthquakes.
- Newport Inglewood Fault — Runs directly through the Los
  Angeles Basin near Huntington Beach. Generated the 1933
  Long Beach earthquake (M6.4).
- Palos Verdes Fault — Offshore fault system capable of
  generating tsunamis affecting the Southern California coast.

The combination of offshore fault systems and the Pacific
subduction zone to the north means Huntington Beach has
the greatest seismic wave generation potential of all
4 locations giving it a perfect seismic score of 100.00.

SURF CITY USA
-------------
Huntington Beach earned the nickname Surf City USA through
decades of surf culture and competition. The US Open of
Surfing held annually at Huntington Beach is one of the
largest surf competitions in the world drawing hundreds of
thousands of spectators each year. The city has a surf
museum a Walk of Fame for surfers and a strong local
surf industry presence.
