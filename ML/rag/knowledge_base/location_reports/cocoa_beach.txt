GEOGRAPHIC CONTEXT
------------------
Cocoa Beach sits on Florida's Space Coast on the Atlantic Ocean
directly east of Cape Canaveral. Unlike Pensacola Beach on the
Gulf of Mexico Cocoa Beach is exposed to the open Atlantic Ocean
giving it greater fetch for wave generation. The Atlantic Ocean
allows swells to travel long distances before reaching the coast
which can produce longer period and higher quality waves than
the Gulf of Mexico.

METEOROLOGICAL DRIVERS
-----------------------
Two primary weather systems drive wave activity at Cocoa Beach:

1. Atlantic Hurricanes — The Atlantic hurricane season runs
June through November. Hurricanes tracking up the East Coast
or making landfall in Florida generate significant swells at
Cocoa Beach. The open Atlantic exposure means swells can build
over longer distances than Gulf locations.

2. Nor'easters — Winter storms called nor'easters track up
the East Coast generating strong northeast winds and significant
wave activity. These storms can produce some of the largest
non-hurricane waves at Cocoa Beach during winter months.

HIGHEST WAVE EVENT
------------------
Cocoa Beach recorded the highest single wave of all 4 locations
in the pipeline at 9.74 meters (31.96 feet) reaching 37.18% of
the Nazaré benchmark. This event likely occurred during a major
hurricane or nor'easter passage when Atlantic swells were at
their maximum.

SEISMIC ACTIVITY
----------------
Seismic activity near Cocoa Beach is extremely minimal. Only 1
earthquake event above magnitude 4.0 was recorded within 500km
during the study period. The Atlantic coast of Florida is far
from major tectonic plate boundaries making seismic wave
generation virtually impossible. This low seismic score
significantly impacts the overall surf potential ranking.

HISTORICAL SURF CULTURE
------------------------
Cocoa Beach has a strong surf culture and is home to Ron Jon
Surf Shop one of the most famous surf shops in the world.
The Ron Jon Easter Surfing Festival has been held at Cocoa
Beach since 1964 making it one of the longest running surf
competitions in the United States.
